Privacy Policy

Last updated September 7, 2026

This policy covers a personal application operated by the owner of this site for their own, exclusive use. It is not offered as a service to anyone else, has no other users, and collects nothing from visitors to this site.

What the application does with Google user data

With the owner's authorization through Google's OAuth consent flow, the application accesses the owner's own Google account in order to:

  • read and send email in the owner's Gmail account, on the owner's behalf and at the owner's request;
  • read the owner's Google Calendar, Contacts, Drive, Sheets and Docs to answer the owner's own questions and carry out tasks the owner asks for.

The only Google account the application touches is the owner's own. No other person's account is accessed, and no other person can use the application.

Storage and sharing

  • Data retrieved from Google APIs is processed and stored on private infrastructure controlled by the owner, not on shared or commercial hosting.
  • Google user data is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising.
  • Message content may be processed by an AI model provider strictly to generate the responses the owner asked for, as part of the application's core functionality, and is not retained by the application beyond what serving the owner requires.
  • This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Limited Use disclosure

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete any stored data at any time, and can revoke the application's access entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates its credentials.
